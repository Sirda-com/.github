### Surface treatment. Built for production.

**SirDa designs and manufactures shot blasting and surface cleaning equipment for steel plates, profiles, castings and industrial components.** From standalone machines to integrated production lines, we help match the cleaning process to your material, part geometry and production requirements.

西尔达专注于抛丸清理与表面处理设备。从钢板、型材到铸件与工业零部件，从独立设备到集成生产线，以工件和生产需求为起点，匹配合适的清理方案。
